4. Add: Three-Tier Impact Verification Contract (`ImpactAssessment`) separating `theoretical_impact`, `observed_impact`, and `not_yet_tested_impact`.
   Evidence: Reasoned from reading `aegis/models.py:127` (`Finding.severity`) and `packages/reporting/reports.py:180-285`. Currently, findings carry a single static `severity` string and reports do not distinguish between the theoretical maximum impact of a vulnerability class, the actual impact demonstrated during authorized validation/session boundary checks, and impact vectors that were intentionally not tested due to engagement boundaries.

   Proposal: Add `ImpactAssessment` (`drafts/gemini/validation_session_engine.py`) linked to `Finding` and rendered into JSON/Markdown/HTML reports in `packages/reporting/reports.py`.
   Verify by: `.venv/bin/python -m pytest drafts/gemini/test_validation_session_engine.py -k test_impact_verification`
